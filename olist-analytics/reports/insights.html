Method

How this was built

Data: Kaggle olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce — 9 CSVs, 1.55M rows. SQL (DuckDB): staging (typing, review de-duplication, ZIP centroids from 1M geolocation points) → star schema (4 dims, 3 facts) → analytical marts (cohorts, freight lanes, seller-month) → 12 automated data-quality tests that block export on failure. Python: pandas + statsmodels logistic regression, Plotly charts. Power BI: PBIP project with TMDL semantic model (relationships, DAX measures, time intelligence) and a 5-page PBIR report.

Definitions: revenue = item price of orders not canceled/unavailable; late = delivered after the estimated date shown at checkout; customer = customer_unique_id.
